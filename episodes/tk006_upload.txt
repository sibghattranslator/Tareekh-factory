Long before written history, caravans carried blue lapis lazuli out of the Afghan mountains to Egypt and Mesopotamia. Who lived here, what do Aryana, Bactria and the Avesta really mean, and where does evidence end and tradition begin? Both sides of the debates, kept apart.
